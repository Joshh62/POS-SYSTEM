# ProfitTrack Privacy-Minimized Adoption Review

## Purpose

This weekly review helps the Founder & CEO distinguish tenant registration,
setup, first value, recent use and commercial follow-up without exposing
customer, product, cashier, payment, revenue or transaction-level information.

## Metric definitions

| Signal | Definition |
|---|---|
| Registered | A tenant business record exists |
| Setup ready | The tenant has at least one branch and one user |
| First value | The tenant has at least one completed sale |
| Active in 7 days | At least one completed sale in the previous 7 days |
| Active in 30 days | At least one completed sale in the previous 30 days |
| Monitor onboarding | No completed sale and tenant age is under 7 days |
| Onboarding follow-up | No completed sale and tenant age is at least 7 days |
| Reactivation follow-up | First value exists but no completed sale in 30 days |

Voided, refunded or otherwise non-completed sales do not establish first value
or recent activity.

## Weekly operating review

1. Open the superadmin Businesses page.
2. Review the five aggregate counts: registered, setup ready, first value,
   active in 30 days and follow-up due.
3. Review only the coarse follow-up label on each tenant card.
4. Choose a commercial action without copying customer or transaction data into
   the corporate library.
5. Record an aggregate weekly signal and action in PACL-011.
6. Update PACL-009 only when the underlying evidence capability changes.

## Boundaries

The view is operational guidance, not proof of customer satisfaction, retention,
revenue, product-market fit or control effectiveness. It does not show funnel
history across prior weeks until repeated review records exist. It does not
automatically promote any PACL-007 control to E4.
